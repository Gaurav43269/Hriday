Welcome to Leonida

The game is set in the state of Leonida, a fictionalized stretch of Florida built around a reimagined Vice City. Rockstar has described it as the biggest and most detailed world it has built for the series, spanning the neon core of the city out into surrounding towns, swamps, and coastline.

The August 2026 "Extended Look" was the most detailed footage shown so far — captured entirely in-engine on PS5 — and gave the clearest picture yet of how the city, its outskirts, and its people fit together.

Two leads

The story follows a couple whose partnership drives the whole narrative.

Jason

One half of the couple at the center of the story. Rockstar has framed the narrative around the pressure of trying to get ahead in a world stacked against him.

Lucia

The series' first playable woman protagonist in the 3D-era mainline games. Her arc has been positioned as equally central to the story, not a side character.

How we got here

The date moved twice before landing where it is now.

December 2023
First trailer
Rockstar's debut trailer confirmed the game and pointed to a 2025 release window, without a specific date.
May 2025
First firm date
Rockstar set May 26, 2026 as the release date — the first exact day attached to the game.
Late 2025
Pushed to November
On a Take-Two earnings call, the date shifted to November 19, 2026, with the extra months put toward polish.
June 25, 2026
Pre-orders open
Pre-orders went live worldwide with the November date holding steady.
August 27, 2026
Extended Look
A full in-engine gameplay reveal, the deepest look yet at Jason, Lucia, and the wider state of Leonida.
